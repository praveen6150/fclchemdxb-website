About Falcon Chemicals (L.L.C.)

Falcon Chemicals (L.L.C.) - Delivering Excellence in Chemical Manufacturing

Established in 1976 by a prominent international business group with a strong presence in basic and specialty chemicals, Falcon Chemicals (L.L.C.) has emerged as a distinguished name in the United Arab Emirates. Over the years, the company has diversified across various business sectors and built a robust marketing network that spans the Middle East, Africa, the Indian subcontinent, and the Far East.

Situated in a state-of-the-art custom-built facility in Jebel Ali Industrial Area, Dubai, Falcon Chemicals (L.L.C.) stands as a leading multi-divisional manufacturing enterprise. We take pride in our ISO 9001 and 14001 certifications, reflecting our commitment to quality and environmental standards. Additionally, our G.C.C. duty exemption certification enables us to import our products duty exempted across the G.C.C. market, providing cost-effective solutions to our valued customers.
